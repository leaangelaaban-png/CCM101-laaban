# Mission Reflection

This laboratory activity helped me see that choosing a cloud platform involves more than comparing the number of services each provider offers. AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities because they all provide services for computing, storage, networking, identity management, databases, and Kubernetes. However, I learned that their individual strengths can make one platform more appropriate than another depending on the situation.

Among the three providers, Microsoft Azure impressed me the most. I found its connection with Microsoft technologies interesting because many organizations already use Microsoft products in their daily operations. I would personally choose Azure because I want to understand how existing systems can be connected or gradually moved to a cloud environment without completely changing the technologies an organization already uses.

The client scenarios showed me why business requirements are important when selecting a cloud provider. A startup, university, research company, and global online business have different priorities. Their existing technology, workload, expected growth, and technical needs should be considered before deciding which cloud platform fits them.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to look at the situation first and recommend services based on the client's needs instead of simply choosing my preferred provider. The KillerCoda investigation also gave me practical experience examining a Linux server and identifying its operating system, processor, memory, and storage resources.

My GitHub portfolio has improved because my activities are now organized with research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, and practical results. It gives me a clear record of the tasks I have completed and helps me see how my knowledge of cloud computing has developed throughout the laboratory activities.
